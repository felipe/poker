# Ultimate Poker odds

Generated by `node scripts/ultimate-odds.js`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the script.

**Rules.** Each player gets 3 hole cards. Five community cards come flop (3), turn (1), river (1), as in Hold'em. Your hand is the best five of your eight cards, using any mix of hole and board. Every three is wild. Five of a kind is the top hand, above a straight flush. A wild can copy a card you already hold, except in a flush, where it becomes the highest card of that suit you don't have.

The standard ranking is kept, but some hands are more common than hands ranked below them:

- Two pair (18.81%) is more common than one pair (15.12%)

- Full house (14.26%) is more common than three of a kind (9.40%)
- Full house (14.26%) is more common than flush (10.18%)

A wild always makes the strongest hand it can, so a hand holding one often jumps past three of a kind to a straight, flush or full house. That leaves three of a kind rarer than the hands ranked just above it. Two pair beats one pair on frequency for a different reason: with eight cards, two pairs show up often even without wilds. Home games keep the standard order rather than reranking by rarity, which would change with every choice of wild rank.
